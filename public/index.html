Apps are suspended after 30 seconds in the background unless they maintain audio playback or graphics activity. Apps can listen for aetheros:suspend and aetheros:resume.

Host memory protection

Normally, critical host memory pressure ends active sessions so users can sign in again. Resist Logging Off keeps this account signed in while AetherOS recovers by stopping its own high-memory daemons and throttling remaining managed daemons. It cannot terminate unrelated operating-system processes.
